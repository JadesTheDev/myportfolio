4D Tesseract Explorer

A tesseract is the four-dimensional analogue of a cube. This page calculates all 16 vertices in four-dimensional space, rotates them through W, projects them into 3D, and then projects that result onto your 2D screen.

4D Rotation

How to use it

Drag the tesseract to rotate the 3D camera. Use the three sliders to rotate the object through planes that include the fourth spatial axis, W.

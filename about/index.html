A little about me.

I’m a software development student who loves figuring out how systems fit together. My path here took a few turns, and my curiosity follows me into nature, creative hobbies, and life with two very opinionated cats.

Read my story

Since I was little, I’ve always liked taking things apart to understand how the pieces work together. Learning how to put them back correctly took a little longer. I usually broke things...

School wasn’t always a good fit for me. With severe ADHD, the traditional classroom often made learning harder than it needed to be. In my junior year of high school, I finally had access to a mechatronics program, and I fell in love. Suddenly, I could explore the systems I’d always been curious about.

My path into programming wasn’t straightforward. After a few attempts at college and some difficult years finding my footing, I wanted a career I genuinely enjoyed that could also give me stability and independence. Programming gave me something to work toward and plenty of things to take apart.

Systems are what keep me hooked. I love seeing separate pieces finally fit together, tracking down a stubborn problem, and refining the small details that make something work well. I can lose an entire afternoon to a project without noticing. Occasionally, the afternoon takes the evening with it.

I’m a perfectionist, which is both a strength and something I’m learning to manage. I care about getting things right, but I’m also learning when to step back, prioritize, and finish. Deadlines have a way of bringing my planning into focus. Sometimes that means a last-minute burst of my best work, though I’m learning to give myself more breathing room.

Outside of tech, I love nature and the simplicity of life away from a screen. My dream is a self-sufficient homestead, a remote career, and enough time for a garden and chickens. For now, my world revolves around two cats: the sassy tortoiseshell Toffee “Mocha” Cattuccino and the house panther CoffeeBean, Lord of Chaos and Snuggles.

I follow my curiosity into watercolor, crochet, embroidery, woodworking, and writing. I’m candid, curious, and usually juggling a few interests. I’m as genuine as it gets. What you see is what you get, with a little humor and probably some cat hair and too much ranting about something random.

About My Work

Software Development

My coursework spans C, C#, Python, SQL, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. I focus on design and troubleshooting, use AI assistance for implementation, and am building my independent coding skills.

More about my work

I like understanding how separate pieces fit together into a working system. My coursework has introduced me to C, C#, Python, SQL, and JavaScript, along with HTML and CSS for web development. My projects include a survival simulator, a weather-data application, and a local-farm discovery app.

My strongest contributions so far are project design and troubleshooting: deciding how something should work, testing it, and tracking down what needs fixing. I’ve used AI assistance extensively for code implementation, and I’m working on strengthening my ability to write, explain, and maintain that code independently.

Systems + DevOps

I’m developing skills in Linux, networking, and cloud applications. My home lab runs Ollama and Open WebUI as I work toward hosting my own AI.

More about my work

Systems are the direction I want to grow in. I’m interested in what happens behind an application: how it runs, how its parts communicate, and how to keep it working reliably.

My coursework includes operating systems, networking, computer configuration, and Linux administration. I’m currently studying cloud native application development and exploring deployment through my projects. I run Ollama and Open WebUI in my home lab, where I plan to host my own AI.

I’m still building experience in this area, but connecting software and infrastructure is what keeps pulling me in.

User-Focused Design

I design around clear navigation, practical layouts, and each owner’s personality. Megan’s and LaShay’s websites show that approach, with AI-assisted code implementation.

More about my work

I care about how a project feels to the person using it. Can they find what they need? Is the next step clear? Does it work comfortably on their phone? Those details shape my design decisions.

For Megan’s harp website and LaShay’s photography website, I directed the layouts, colors, typography, and visual details to reflect each person’s work. I used AI assistance to implement the code, then tested the results and worked through revisions.

I enjoy refining those small details, but the goal is always a site that represents its owner and makes sense to its visitors.

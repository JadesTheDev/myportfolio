About me

Developer, systems thinker, professional tinkerer.

Software DevelopmentWeb DevelopmentSystemsDevOpsUI/UXProblem Solving

I like building things that make sense.

I’m a software development student whose work sits at the intersection of programming, systems, and user experience. I’m interested in more than making an application technically function; I want the person on the other side of the screen to understand it, trust it, and actually enjoy using it.

My background has given me experience with both the technical and practical sides of technology. I’ve worked with web interfaces, application development, systems concepts, deployment workflows, and team projects, and I’m continuing to expand that range as I move through my degree.

I’m especially drawn to useful software: tools for communities, small organizations, everyday workflows, and problems where a thoughtful interface can remove friction instead of adding more of it.

I also enjoy the experimental side of development. Some projects begin as coursework, some begin as strange ideas that refuse to leave me alone, and the good ones usually teach me something I didn’t expect.
